Impressiva Printing, custom print shop in Pasadena

Custom printing for businesses and brands, from business cards to building-size banners, produced quickly and finished to a high standard.

Every job is proofed before it runs and checked by hand before it leaves the studio.

What The Studio Prints

Press And Paper

  • Business Cards

    Soft-touch and spot-gloss stocks in 16 and 32pt, with optional foil and spot UV.

  • Flyers and Posters

    Full-bleed flyers and posters on 100lb gloss or matte stock, printed both sides.

  • Packaging

    Custom boxes, mailers and labels on SBS board, produced in small batches.

Wide Format And Apply

  • Vinyl Banners

    Weatherproof 13oz vinyl, hemmed and grommeted, in sizes up to ten feet wide.

  • Custom Apparel

    DTF and screen-printed tees, hoodies, caps and team apparel, printed in full color.

  • Stickers and Signs

    Die-cut vinyl, plus coroplast, aluminum and foam board for storefront signage.

How A Job Runs

Send artwork as a print-ready PDF, PNG, SVG or 300dpi JPG with a 0.125 inch bleed.

The studio preflights the file, returns a proof for approval, and only then puts the job on press.

Standard runs ship in three to five business days. Rush work goes same-day when files arrive approved before noon.

Small quantities are welcome: fifty stickers, one hundred cards, or a single banner.

Stocks And Finishes

Stocks run from 14pt card through 32pt board, 13oz and 18oz vinyl, cotton apparel and SBS packaging board.

Finishes include soft-touch lamination, spot UV, foil, die-cutting and rounded corners.

The floor runs offset, digital, screen, direct-to-film and wide-format presses under one roof.

Color is CMYK plus spot inks, calibrated to G7 and proofed by hand before every run.
